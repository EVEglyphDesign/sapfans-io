# Inbox — paste links here

Anything you find: a LinkedIn post, a gist, a session, a blog. One per line, a few words
after it if you like. Or paste it into any AI session working on this repo and say
"add to SAPfans references". Or open a [Suggest a reference](https://github.com/EVEglyphDesign/sapfans-io/issues/new?template=reference.yml) issue.

The ARK sweep reads this file, queues each line with `scripts/refs.py add`, and clears
what it queued into the [review list](../registry/PROPOSALS.md). What it misses here teaches
the next sweep where to look.
