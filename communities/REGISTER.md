## Conventions

- **Append only.** Never edit a row after it is committed. If the disposition changes, add a new row citing the previous one.
- **Kind:**
  - **PR** — a pull request we opened against an upstream repository.
  - **Issue** — a substantive issue with a reproducible finding.
  - **Sample** — a public repository of ours that another community links to or uses.
  - **Talk** — a conference, meetup, or user-group session.
  - **Citation** — a place where the [Additive Doctrine](../paper/ADDITIVE-DOCTRINE.md) or another SAPfans.io artefact has been named in someone else's work.
- **Disposition:** the honest state on the last known date. If we do not know, write `unknown` and the date checked.

## Why this exists

An estate's actual standing in the commons is the sum of the artefacts it has given, not the essays it has published on its own surface. The [Additive Doctrine](../paper/ADDITIVE-DOCTRINE.md) claims that this estate is provider-neutral and community-additive; this register is the evidence, or the absence of it.
