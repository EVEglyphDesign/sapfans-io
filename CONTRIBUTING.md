# Contributing

**This doctrine is expanded by practitioners.** There is no editorial board and no
credential requirement. If you have shipped something and learned something, you qualify.

## The one rule

> **Append, correct, supersede — never delete.**

The record is the asset. A correction is a new paragraph naming what it corrects, or a new
file with a `SUPERSEDED-BY` line on the old one. Erasure is the only unforgivable edit,
because it is the failure that destroyed the original SAPfans archive — see
[Heritage](heritage/HERITAGE.md).

## What is welcome

- **Field corrections.** Something here is wrong, and you can show it.
- **Additions to the pattern.** A rule that earned its place by costing you something.
- **Provider-neutral technique.** Extraction, custody, portability, twin construction.
- **Translations.** French especially.
- **Heritage.** Verifiable history of the independent SAP practitioner community, with
  sources.

## What is not

- **Client material.** In any form, however anonymised it feels.
- **Credentials, keys, tokens, connection strings.** Not once, not briefly.
- **Attacks on named engineers, practitioners or service providers.** Critique provisions and
  posture; never people or their employers.
- **Marketing.** No product pitches, no partnership tiers, no lead capture.
- **Anything you cannot source.** Assertions about the world need a citation. Assertions
  about your own experience need to be labelled as such.

## How

1. Fork, branch, commit with a message that says what changed and why.
2. Open a pull request describing what you are correcting or adding, and how you know.
3. Expect questions about provenance. They are not hostility; they are the standard.

Corrections to fact are merged quickly. Changes to doctrine are slower, because a doctrine
that changes on every pull request is not one.

## Tone

Firm, precise, and **non-hostile**. This is an invitation to an industry, not a campaign
against one. If a sentence would make a working SAP architect defensive rather than
curious, rewrite it.
