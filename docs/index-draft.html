The base model

What you get when you fork.

A one-file starter that becomes your own repository. Three parts, in the same order every time.

  1. Boot contractThe rules your AI reads first. Cheapest-source-first, no wasted spend, no cold starts.
  2. CanonThe house standards. Palette, naming, format, tone — so a team's outputs stay coherent.
  3. Sin registryThe honest record of mistakes. What went wrong, when, and what the cheaper path was.
Human-review gate. Contributors run AI on transcripts and file structured feedback. An owner consolidates, drafts a version bump, and a human signs off before it ships.

Community norms
