EgD-SAPF-002 · Sovereign Start

The most basic boundary
you can put around an AI surface.

Three edges. One repository. Downloadable as one Markdown file. Drop it into a new GitHub repo and your AI surface has guardrails it did not have twenty minutes earlier.

Download SOVEREIGN-STARTER.md Read the landing

The operator on one side of the repository, the objective on the other. The triangle in between binds every token of the model's processing to the axis between them — boot contract at entry, canon at exit, sin registry logging every attempt to leave that axis.

The three edges

1.Boot contract

How the AI surface behaves before it does anything. The read-first rule. The one edge every session starts on.

2.Canon

How the artifact leaves the repository. The output shape. The edge the work travels along on the way out.

3.Sin registry

How the truth of what happened is preserved. The record of what was done wrong so it can be done right. The edge that closes the triangle.

Take the boot contract away and the surface drifts. Take the canon away and the artifact leaks. Take the sin registry away and the other two become preferences instead of rules.

Why bother

The AI surface is a web browser. Nothing you say to it is permanent. Nothing you reason inside it comes back to you once the session closes.

Facebook is beginning to give people their history back. AI surfaces are not promising that yet. Until they do, the only way to keep what you think is to keep it somewhere you hold. You hold the repository. That is the whole idea. Everything else is mechanics.

This scaffold is not the sophisticated version. It is not the enterprise version. It is not the controlled EVEglyphDesign canon. It is the minimum shape that still works. More guardrails can be added on top later. The three edges are the floor.

How this connects to the doctrine

SAPfans.io asks for the right to add: to mirror the ledger read-only into a store the customer owns, and to build on top of it. The pattern is provider-neutral — fork the repo, point your AI at the record, build one twin. That step assumes you already have a repository your AI reads and writes against, and an operating shape that keeps its output honest. If you do not, the pattern does not land.

Sovereign Start is the floor beneath the pattern. It gives a first-time practitioner a repository, a boot contract, an output canon, and a sin registry in one file — enough shape that an AI surface can be pointed at their record without producing toilet paper. Once the floor is in, the pattern becomes usable.
